Clutch Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026 · applies to the Clutch beta for iOS

The short version: what you log in Clutch (your periods, moods, symptoms and notes) stays on your phone. We don't sell your data, we don't use it for ads, and we can't see your cycle.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you log: period days and flow, mood, energy, symptoms and what helped.
  • What you tell Clutch while setting up, like your name, age and usual cycle length.
  • The predictions, insights and doctor summary Clutch works out from those logs.
  • Your private journal entries.
  • History you choose to bring in from another app (see "Importing your history" below).

This data is stored only on your device. It isn't uploaded to our servers. If you delete the app, it's deleted with it.

What leaves your phone

  • An anonymous account. Clutch signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase. This creates a random ID with no name, email or phone number attached.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose to sign in, Firebase keeps the identifier Apple gives us and, if you share it, your name and email (Apple lets you hide your email). It's used only so you can get your account back on a new phone. Your logs still stay on your phone.
  • Free period product reports. When you tap "Report empty" or "It's stocked" on a spot, we store which spot, which report, your anonymous ID and the time, so other people see whether it's stocked. Nothing about your cycle is included.
  • App usage statistics. Clutch uses Google Firebase Analytics to see general usage, like how many people open the app, which version they're on, and their device model and country. It doesn't include anything you log about your cycle. Advertising features are turned off, and the data isn't used for ads.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends us a technical report (what the app was doing, your device model and iOS version) so we can fix it. It never includes what you log.
  • Beta feedback. If you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple shares it, and any screenshot you choose to attach, with us.

Permissions Clutch asks for

  • Location, only while the app is open, to sort free period product spots by distance. Your location is used on your phone and isn't stored or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications, for reminders you choose to set. They're scheduled on your phone.
  • Photos (add only), to save a bouquet image when you ask it to. Clutch can't see your photo library.
  • Apple Health (optional, read only), to bring in your past periods and some symptoms (like cramps, bloating and headaches) that other apps such as Clue or Flo have saved there. Clutch never writes to Apple Health. What it reads stays on your phone, and it is never used for advertising or marketing, or shared with anyone.

You can change any of these in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

Importing your history

You can bring in history from another period tracker, either from Apple Health or by picking your data download from Clue. Clutch reads the file on your phone and keeps only your period days, moods and the symptoms Clutch tracks. Nothing is uploaded. You can undo an import at any time.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. The only companies involved are Google (Firebase and Crashlytics, as described above) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, Apple Health and TestFlight). When you share something from Clutch, like a bouquet to Instagram, you choose what goes and where.

Health information

Clutch isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything. Its predictions and checks are estimates based on what you log. Talk to a doctor about any health concerns.

Age

Clutch is meant for people 15 and older.

Deleting your data

In Clutch, tap your name, then "Delete my account and data". That deletes your account from our servers and erases everything Clutch keeps on your phone. Deleting the app also removes everything you've logged; to remove your account too, use that button first or email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Clutch is in beta, and if a future version stores any of your data online, we'll say so here and in the app first.
