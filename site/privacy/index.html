Clutch Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026 · applies to the Clutch beta for iOS

The short version: what you log in Clutch (your periods, moods, symptoms and notes) stays on your phone. We don't sell your data, we don't use it for ads, and we can't see your cycle.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you log: period days and flow, mood, energy, symptoms and what helped.
  • What you tell Clutch while setting up, like your name, age and usual cycle length.
  • The predictions, insights and doctor summary Clutch works out from those logs.

This data is stored only on your device. It isn't uploaded to our servers. If you delete the app, it's deleted with it.

What leaves your phone

  • An anonymous account. Clutch signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase so the app can check it's connected. This creates a random ID with no name, email or phone number attached, and stores only that ID and when it last checked in.
  • App usage statistics. Clutch uses Google Firebase Analytics to see general usage, like how many people open the app, which version they're on, and their device model and country. It doesn't include anything you log about your cycle. Advertising features are turned off, and the data isn't used for ads.
  • Beta crash and feedback reports. If you send feedback or the app crashes, Apple's TestFlight shares that report, and any screenshot you choose to attach, with us.

Permissions Clutch asks for

  • Location, only while the app is open, to sort free period product spots by distance. Your location is used on your phone and isn't stored or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications, for reminders you choose to set. They're scheduled on your phone.
  • Photos (add only), to save a bouquet image when you ask it to. Clutch can't see your photo library.

You can change any of these in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. The only companies involved are Google (Firebase, as described above) and Apple (TestFlight). When you share something from Clutch, like a bouquet to Instagram, you choose what goes and where.

Health information

Clutch isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything. Its predictions and checks are estimates based on what you log. Talk to a doctor about any health concerns.

Age

Clutch is meant for people 13 and older.

Deleting your data

Deleting Clutch from your phone removes everything you've logged. To have your anonymous ID removed from our servers too, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Clutch is in beta, and if a future version stores any of your data online, we'll say so here and in the app first.
